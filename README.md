# CampusConnect-Android

An Android application for connecting students and club managers on campus, built with Jetpack Compose and MVI Architecture.

## 📱 Features

### 🎓 For Students
* **Home Feed:** Stay updated with the latest posts and announcements with seamless interactions.
* **Club Explorer:** Discover university clubs and view detailed club profiles.
* **Event Management:** Browse upcoming events, view details, and keep track of campus activities.
* **Facility Reservation:** Easily book study rooms and sports facilities on campus.
* **Issue Reporting:** Quickly report campus issues directly through the app.
* **User Profile & Reservations:** Manage your personal profile and keep track of your reservations (with easy cancellation support).
* **Notifications:** Stay informed about important updates and activities.

### 👔 For Club Managers
* **Club Dashboard:** Centralized overview of club activities and metrics.
* **Event Scheduling:** Create, schedule, and manage club events.
* **Attendance Management:** Streamlined batch submission system for event check-ins.
* **Post Management:** Create, delete, and manage club announcements in the newsfeed.
* **Requests Handling:** Easily manage student requests.
* **Account Management:** Update and maintain club profile details.
