## The Vision

CampusConnect bridges the gap between vibrant student life and efficient club management. Built on modern Android standards with **Jetpack Compose** and an **MVI architecture**, it guarantees a responsive, state-driven, and beautiful experience that adapts perfectly to your device with dynamic **Material You** themes!

---

## Standout Features

### For the Students
* **Dynamic Newsfeed:** Stay in the loop with the latest campus announcements, boasting optimistic UI for instant like and comment feedback.
* **Club Explorer:** Discover hidden gems and popular university clubs with rich, detailed profiles.
* **Event Hub:** Browse, track, and register for upcoming events and workshops with ease.
* **Facility Booking:** Secure study rooms or sports facilities on campus in just a few taps.
* **Quick Issue Reporting:** Report campus issues to the administration seamlessly.
* **Smart Notifications:** Never miss a beat with real-time updates on your activities and reservations.

### For the Managers
* **Command Dashboard:** A centralized, birds-eye view of all club operations and engagement metrics.
* **Effortless Scheduling:** Create and manage high-impact club events.
* **Batch Attendance:** Streamline event check-ins with our powerful batch submission system.
* **Post Management:** Broadcast your club's voice directly to the student newsfeed.
* **Request Handling:** Manage incoming student requests without breaking a sweat.
